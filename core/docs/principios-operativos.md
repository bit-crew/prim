# Operating Principles — Generalist Company

Principles applicable to any company or project seeking maximum efficiency with minimum complexity.

---

## 1. Operational Minimalism

Administration must tend toward zero.

- No payroll or expenses not tied to revenue
- No in-kind benefits to partners or employees
- Every benefit is exclusively monetary (dividends, bonuses)
- If a process can be eliminated without affecting revenue, eliminate it
- If a process can be automated, automate it before hiring

---

## 2. Technological Governance

The company bases its management on systems that minimize operational human dependency.

### Technological principles

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| Immutable Traceability | Every movement is recorded in auditable logs |
| Single Source of Truth | All information lives in one place (no duplicates) |
| Process Automation | Repetitive manual processes are avoided |
| Remote and Asynchronous Management | Assets are managed without physical presence |

### Technological priorities

1. Packages/libraries over running services
2. Plain text over databases (when sufficient)
3. Automation over hiring
4. CLI over GUI

---

## 3. Investment Rule: ROE

Every investment must be based on numbers and profitability, not personal preference.

```
ROE = (Net Income - Debt Payments) / Own Capital x 100
```

### Decision criteria

- If the projected ROE does not beat the lower-risk alternative, do not invest
- The calculation must be documented before acquisition
- Periodic review: if the actual ROE diverges >20% from projected, evaluate exit

---

## 4. Political Immunity Principle

Investment prioritizes assets immune to central-government decisions:
- Interest rates
- Inflation
- Money printing
- Capital controls

Seek goods that retain or gain value independently of local monetary policy. This principle takes priority over pure ROE.

---

## 5. Debt Rule

| Debt Level | Action |
|---|---|
| Liabilities > 10% of Net Equity | Plan to reduce to 0% within 12 months |
| Liabilities > 30% of Net Equity | Requires approval of 90% of subscribed shares |

Exception: A Global Economic Emergency State allows extending the deadline to 24 months to avoid a forced sale at depressed prices.

---

## 6. Investment in Quality (OPEX vs CAPEX)

When acquiring assets, aim to reach a state of **low or zero maintenance** over low cost.

- Assets requiring continuous management, recurring repairs, or external dependencies are not accepted
- If an asset has latent defects but good profitability: a budgeted plan to reach low maintenance within 180 days
- For financial investments: verifiable liquidity and low operating cost

---

## Application

These principles apply to any instance (company, personal project, portfolio). The specific instance defines:
- Which assets it owns
- Which investment strategy it follows
- Which risk threshold it accepts

Prim provides the rules. The instance provides the context.

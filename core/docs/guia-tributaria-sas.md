# Tax Guide — SAS Colombia

Generalist guide for the tax management of a Simplified Shares Company (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada, SAS) in Colombia.

> Note: Colombia-specific legal/tax terms are kept in their original form: DIAN (tax authority), SAS, UVT (Tax Value Unit), SOAT (mandatory vehicle insurance), ICA (Industry & Commerce tax), RUB (Ultimate Beneficial Owners registry).

---

## STEP 1: ACQUISITION

How an asset enters the company.

| Scenario | Mechanism | Tax Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Asset the shareholder already owned | In-Kind Contribution (ownership transferred in exchange for shares) | No income tax on sale |
| New asset | Direct Purchase (the SAS buys; the SAS is the owner) | Electronic invoice in the name of the SAS |

**Golden Rule:** The invoice or deed must always be in the name of the **SAS**, never in the shareholder's personal name.

---

## STEP 2: CLASSIFICATION BY VALUE (50 UVT Rule)

| Asset Value | Accounting Classification | Treatment | Management |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 50 UVT (~$2,353,000) | EXPENSE (Deductible) | 100% deductible in year 1 | Digital invoice; no fiscal inventory |
| > 50 UVT (~$2,353,000) | FIXED ASSET | Deductible via annual depreciation (10-20%) | Capitalize in accounting; write-off record when retired |

**Optimization:** If a set of goods exceeds 50 UVT but each individual item does not, request a separate invoice per item. Each is deducted as an immediate expense.

---

## STEP 3: JUSTIFICATION BEFORE THE DIAN

The asset must be assigned to one of these productive centers (Art. 107, Tax Statute):

| Cost Center | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Economic Exploitation | Assets to generate revenue | Furniture for rental, showroom |
| Asset Appreciation | Improvements that increase commercial appraisal | Renovations, major repairs |
| Management Tools | Technology for digital administration | Computer, phone, tablet, software |

On the invoice or contract, record the purpose. In accounting, assign it to the corresponding account.

---

## STEP 4: TAX BENEFIT (Fiscal Deduction)

| Asset/Expense Type | Deduction | When It Applies |
|---|---|---|
| Asset < 50 UVT | 100% in year 1 | Year of purchase |
| Asset > 50 UVT | Annual depreciation 10-20% | Spread over following years |
| Associated Operating Expenses | 100% deductible ALWAYS | Year incurred |

### Associated Operating Expenses (always 100% deductible)

| Asset Type | Associated Expenses |
|---|---|
| Real estate | Building repairs, utilities, insurance, property tax, security |
| Vehicle | Fuel, SOAT, insurance, tax, maintenance, tires, tolls |
| Furniture/Technology | Maintenance, repair, accessories, software |

---

## STEP 5: ACCOUNTING AND SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS

| Operation Type | Mandatory Support | Accounting Record | Observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset Purchase | Electronic Invoice in the name of the SAS | Expense or Fixed Asset (by value) | Without a valid invoice, no deduction |
| Income (Rent/Lease) | Sales Invoice or Contract | Income Statement | All income must go through the bank |
| Operating Expense | Electronic Invoice | Income Statement | POS/ticket does NOT count; must be an invoice in the name of the SAS |
| Bank Movement | Monthly statement | Statement of Financial Position | Use a corporate card for traceability |

**Golden Rule:** Every movement must go through the bank and be supported by an electronic invoice in the name of the SAS. NO cash, NO third-party invoices.

---

## STEP 6: DIAN REPORTING OBLIGATIONS

| Obligation | Frequency | Approx. Deadline | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Income Tax | Annual | April-May | Return with deductible expenses |
| Exogenous Information | Annual | April-May | Report of all movements, fixed assets, debt |
| Withholding at Source | Monthly (if applicable) | Following month | If the SAS made payments that generate withholding |
| VAT (IVA) | Bimonthly/Quarterly | Per calendar | Only if there are sales with VAT (commercial leases yes; housing NO) |
| ICA (Industry and Commerce) | Annual or Bimonthly | Per municipality | By economic activity |
| RUB (Ultimate Beneficial Owners) | Immediate update | Immediate | Report shareholders |
| Electronic Invoicing | Per transaction | Immediate | Mandatory from $1 |

---

## EXCLUSIVITY RULE: PERSONAL vs OPERATING EXPENSE

So the DIAN does not challenge the expenses:

| Forbidden (Personal Expense) | Allowed (Operating Expense) |
|---|---|
| Personal grocery shopping | Building repair |
| Clothing/shoes (not a uniform) | Utilities of the premises |
| Children's school | Fuel for operational mobility |
| Vacations/tourism | Property tax and SOAT |
| Personal doctor/pharmacy | Vehicle insurance |
| Personal entertainment/streaming | Car maintenance |
| Gym/sports | Technology for administration |

**Criterion:** If the expense is directly tied to the physical structure (real estate), operational mobility (vehicle), or work tools (technology), it is allowed.

## SUMMARY

1. All assets enter the SAS via In-Kind Contribution (existing) or Direct Purchase (new) — invoice always in the name of the SAS
2. If it costs less than 50 UVT, it is an immediate expense (100% deductible year 1); if it costs more, it is depreciated, but its associated expenses are always 100% deductible
3. It is justified by assigning it to a productive center (Rental, Appreciation, or Management), recorded with digital support, and reported annually to the DIAN
